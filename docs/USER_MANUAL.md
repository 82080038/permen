## Getting Started

SKD CAT-BKN is a web-based platform for practicing SKD (Seleksi Kompetensi Dasar) CAT-BKN (Computer Assisted Test) questions. This platform helps you prepare for the CPNS (Calon Pegawai Negeri Sipil) selection process.

### Requirements
- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Stable internet connection
- Valid phone number for registration

## Registration & Login

### Registration
1. Visit the registration page
2. Enter your full name
3. Enter your phone number (must be unique)
4. Create a password (minimum 8 characters)
5. Click "Daftar" to register

### Login
1. Visit the login page
2. Enter your phone number
3. Enter your password
4. Click "Masuk" to login

### Forgot Password
If you forget your password:
1. Click "Lupa Password" on the login page
2. Enter your phone number
3. Follow the instructions to reset your password

## Taking Tryouts

### Starting a Tryout
1. Navigate to the "Tryout" section
2. Select an available tryout event
3. Click "Mulai Tryout" to begin
4. Read the instructions carefully
5. Click "Lanjut" to start the test

### During the Tryout
- **Navigation**: Use the question navigation grid to jump between questions
- **Timer**: Keep an eye on the remaining time
- **Answering**: Select your answer for each question
- **Flagging**: Mark questions you want to review later
- **Review**: Use the "Ragu-ragu" option if you're unsure

### Submitting the Tryout
1. Answer all questions or leave blank if unsure
2. Click "Selesai" when done
3. Review your answers if time permits
4. Confirm submission

### Viewing Results
After submission, you'll see:
- Your total score
- Score breakdown by subtes (TWK, TIU, TKP)
- Comparison with passing grade
- Detailed answer review

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Cannot Login**
- Verify your phone number and password
- Check your internet connection
- Reset your password if needed

**Tryout Not Loading**
- Refresh the page
- Check your internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different browser

**Questions Not Displaying**
- Wait a few seconds for loading
- Refresh the page
- Check your internet connection

**Score Not Saving**
- Ensure you clicked "Selesai" to submit
- Check your internet connection
- Contact support if issue persists

### Getting Help
If you encounter issues not covered here:
- Check the FAQ section
- Contact support via the feedback form
- Join our community forum (if available)

## Tips for Success

1. **Practice Regularly**: Take daily quizzes consistently
2. **Review Mistakes**: Learn from your incorrect answers
3. **Time Management**: Practice with time limits
4. **Focus on Weaknesses**: Identify and improve weak areas
5. **Stay Consistent**: Maintain a study schedule
6. **Use Materials**: Read learning materials before practicing
7. **Simulate Real Test**: Take full tryouts under realistic conditions

## Security Tips

- Keep your password secure
- Don't share your account

- Use a strong password
